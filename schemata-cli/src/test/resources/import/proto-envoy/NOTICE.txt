Nothing in the copied files was changed.

                   checked out on 2026-10-09. Apache License, Version 2.0; see LICENSE.

  include/         xds/core/v3/context_params.proto, xds/core/v3/extension.proto
                   exported with buf from buf.build/cncf/xds (Apache-2.0).
                   google/rpc/status.proto
                   exported with buf from buf.build/googleapis/googleapis (Apache-2.0).
                   The importer reads these as include files: they are reached through imports
                   and emitted, but they are not inputs.
